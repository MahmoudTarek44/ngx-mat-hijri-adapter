Date adapter

HijriDateAdapter implements Material's DateAdapter<CalendarDate> for both calendars.

Configuration

provideHijriDateAdapter() registers the adapter together with MAT_DATE_FORMATS and MAT_DATE_LOCALE. Its options are:

  • calendar: 'gregorian' or 'islamic-umalqura'. Defaults to islamic-umalqura.
  • locale: digits, Gregorian month names, weekday names, and the first day of the week. Defaults to ar-SA.
  • timeZone: used by today(). Defaults to the runtime time zone.
  • formats: Material format slots. Defaults to HIJRI_DATE_FORMATS.

Locale is not the calendar

Calendar and locale are independent. ar-SA does not select the Hijri calendar, en-US does not select Gregorian, and a Hijri calendar does not force Saturday as the week start. In this runtime, en-US and ar-SA start the week on Sunday, and ar-EG starts it on Saturday. setLocale updates those rules and never changes the calendar.

Switching calendars

setCalendar() switches an adapter instance at runtime, and calendar reads the current one. Material redraws through localeChanges. After a switch, the adapter reads, formats, and clones any CalendarDate in its new calendar. Use it on an adapter provided to a single component, as the form fields do.

Month indexing

Material month indexes are 0-based, unlike the calendar helpers. createDate throws for a month outside 0–11, a day outside that month, or an Umm al-Qura year outside 1300–1599. Adding months clamps to the last valid day. Adding a year or month that leaves AH 1300–1599 throws.

Time methods are not implemented.
